Problem Statement

Visit https://naikroop.com/ and https://naikflow.com/ and research our product, NaikFlow, to understand its vision, capabilities, and the problems it aims to solve.

Based on your understanding of Naikflow and your own imagination, design and build an application using any tools, technologies, or AI platforms of your choice.

The objective of this assignment is not to evaluate your expertise in a specific technology or tool.
Instead, we want to understand:

Your imagination and creativity
Your problem-solving approach
Your product thinking and understanding of user needs
How effectively you leverage AI throughout the development process
Your ability to follow an AI-Native SDLC, using AI across ideation, planning, design, development, testing, debugging, documentation, and iteration
Your ability to turn an idea into a working application
Your overall strategy, decision-making, and execution
You are free to choose the problem, application concept, user experience, technology stack, AI tools, and development approach based on your own ideas.

There is no single correct solution. We are interested in seeing how you think, how you use AI and available tools, and how effectively you turn an idea into a working product.

Key Expectation

Follow an AI-Native SDLC throughout the assignment. We encourage you to demonstrate how AI is integrated into your development workflow—not merely for writing code, but as a partner across the entire product development lifecycle.
